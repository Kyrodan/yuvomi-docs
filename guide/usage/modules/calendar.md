# Calendar

## What this module is for

Calendar is the shared schedule for appointments, deadlines and time-based planning across the household. It is designed to bring external calendars together instead of forcing the family into a single isolated calendar silo.

## What people usually do here

- Connect Google or CalDAV calendars and keep Yuvomi in two-way sync with them.
- Create local events, recurring series and one-off exceptions in the same calm interface.
- Subscribe to ICS feeds for schools, clubs, sports or public holidays.
- Search and filter the schedule when several people and calendars overlap.

## How it connects to other modules

- [Family](./family) gives events visible member assignment and avatar context.
- [Birthdays](./birthdays) can generate automatic calendar events from birthday data.
- [Reminders](./reminders) extends calendar reminders beyond the in-app view.
- The dashboard can show today's upcoming appointments together with tasks and meals.

## Good to know

- Event visibility is per item, so private appointments can stay private while shared events remain visible to everyone who needs them.
- Recurring events support the practical edit scopes people expect: this event, this and following, or the whole series.
- Yuvomi can import an ICS file or feed once into editable local events when a permanent subscription is not the right fit.
- A read-only webcal export is available for households that need a simple outward-facing feed.
