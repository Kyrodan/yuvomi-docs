# Health

## What this module is for

Health is the family's private log for recurring health data. It gives each member their own place for vitals, medications, lab values, activity notes and menstrual cycle tracking, while keeping that data on your own server instead of in a third-party health cloud.

## What people usually do here

- Record measurements such as blood pressure, glucose, weight, SpO2 or temperature over time.
- Track medications, planned doses and refill needs.
- Save lab results and simple activity entries so important data stays together.
- Use cycle tracking for period history, fertile-window estimates and pregnancy mode where relevant.

## How it connects to other modules

- [Family](./family) provides the member profiles this module records data for.
- [Reminders](./reminders) can surface medication-related follow-ups and other time-based health tasks.
- The dashboard can highlight recent or due health-related information in the broader daily overview.

## Good to know

- Health data is intentionally per entry and per member, so private information can stay private.
- An admin can allow one member to record for another, which is useful when a parent tracks a child's fever or medication.
- The cycle diary stays excluded from that delegated recording path.
- Yuvomi explicitly documents that Health is not a medical device, so this module is best used as a household record, not as a diagnostic tool.
