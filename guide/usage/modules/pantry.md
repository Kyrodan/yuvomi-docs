# Pantry

## What this module is for

Pantry is the stock view of what is physically in the house. It is useful when a family wants more than a shopping list and needs to know what is already there, what is running low, and what should be used before it expires.

## What people usually do here

- Record items with quantities, units, storage locations and best-before dates.
- Check what is low, empty, expired or expiring soon.
- Adjust quantities quickly when something is used or restocked.
- Push low items to shopping without retyping them.

## How it connects to other modules

- [Shopping](./shopping) is the natural partner for restocking and for booking purchases back in.
- [Meals](./meals) and [Recipes](./recipes) benefit because the pantry helps show what is already available for the week.

## Good to know

- Minimum stock can be stored per item, which makes low-stock filtering much more useful in practice.
- Storage locations can be renamed and sorted without losing the stock assigned to them.
- Items that were checked off after a shop can be booked into the pantry with their quantity and unit, so shopping does not end when you leave the store.
- Pantry is especially useful for households that batch-buy staples, freeze meals or track expiry-sensitive items.
