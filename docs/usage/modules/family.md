# Family

## What this module is for

Family defines who belongs to the household inside Yuvomi. Profiles, roles and photos are not just cosmetic here - they are the shared identity layer that other modules use for assignment, visibility and context.

## What people usually do here

- Add or update member profiles with names, photos and contact details.
- Review who has which role in the household.
- Invite new members so they can choose their own password and join directly.
- Keep personal details aligned with connected contacts and birthdays.

## How it connects to other modules

- [Tasks](./tasks), [Calendar](./calendar) and [Rewards](./rewards) rely on member identities for assignment and attribution.
- [Health](./health) records data per member.
- [Birthdays](./birthdays) and [Contacts](./contacts) can share relevant identity data.

## Good to know

- Invite links let the invited person choose their own password instead of receiving one from an admin.
- Roles matter across the app, especially for administrative features such as [Backup](./backup) or [API Tokens](./api-tokens).
- Yuvomi also adapts to very small households: when there is only one member, several family-specific controls can step out of the way until they are needed.
