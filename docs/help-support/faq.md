# FAQ

## Do I need every module?

No. Most people regularly use only a few modules and ignore the rest.

## Is Yuvomi better in the browser or as an installed app?

For frequent use, installing it to your home screen is usually more convenient. The browser remains the fallback.

## Who can see my entries?

That depends on the module and your household setup. If visibility matters for a specific item, your module guide should explain the exact behavior.

## I was invited but cannot sign in. What now?

Ask your household admin to confirm that:

- your invitation is still valid
- your account exists
- the shared URL is correct

## Can I use Yuvomi on multiple devices?

Yes. A common setup is desktop during planning and phone during the day.

## Where should I report confusing wording or missing docs?

Capture the page, the unclear section, and what you expected to find. That is enough to turn vague feedback into a concrete docs improvement.
